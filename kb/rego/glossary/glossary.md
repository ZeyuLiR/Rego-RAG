Used by the assistant to map Chinese and informal terms to official NSW terms. Add a row
whenever clerks use a term the assistant does not recognise.

| English term | 中文 / 常用说法 | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Registration (rego) | 注册、车辆注册、续牌 | Renewing is "registration renewal". |
| Registration transfer | 过户、转名 | Change of the registered operator. |
| Interstate transfer | 外州转入、外州车转 NSW | Registering a vehicle previously registered in another state. |
| CTP insurance (Green Slip) | 强制第三者保险、绿单 | Compulsory third party insurance. |
| eSafety check (Pink Slip) | 粉单、车检 | Safety inspection for registration renewal of older light vehicles. |
| AUVIS inspection (Blue Slip) | 蓝单、蓝单检查 | Safety and identity inspection, e.g. unregistered or interstate vehicles. |
| Number plates | 车牌、牌照 | |
| Unregistered Vehicle Permit (UVP) | 临时行驶许可 | Permit to drive an unregistered vehicle for a specific purpose. |
| Conditional registration | 有条件注册 | Registration with operating conditions. |
| Motor vehicle duty | 印花税、过户税 | Duty payable on registration or transfer. |
| Service NSW | 服务中心、Service NSW | Where customers transact. |
| Transport for NSW (TfNSW) | 交通部 | The agency responsible for registration rules. |
